# Global 10 mm results

All 21 lines completed. Each numbered line has three files:

- `line_001_Ex.jpg` … `line_021_Ex.jpg`: B-scan images; distance is horizontal and time is vertical.
- `line_001_Ex.npz` … `line_021_Ex.npz`: compressed Ex matrices and Tx/Rx coordinates.
- `line_001_trace_coordinates.csv` … `line_021_trace_coordinates.csv`: numbered trace coordinates.

Parameters: 10 mm Yee grid, 10 ns time window, 21 lines at 100 mm spacing, 301 traces per line at 10 mm spacing, nominal Tx/Rx separation 46 mm, X polarization.

Overview: `global_10mm_overview.jpg`. Complete local package: `archives/Dongzhi-Nan_global_10mm_results_2026-11-14.zip`.
